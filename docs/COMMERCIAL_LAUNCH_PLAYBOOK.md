# NODUS Commercial Launch Playbook — Legacy Direction

> **Superseded by [NODUS Master Business & Product Blueprint](NODUS_MASTER_BLUEPRINT.md).** This playbook describes the earlier shipping-cost reconciliation offer. Retain as historical context only; the new target is recurring 3PL billing and performance control.


**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-11-15

## 1. Commercial position

**Category:** Shopify shipping-cost reconciliation.

**One-line value proposition:** NODUS connects Shopify order shipping revenue with merchant-provided carrier and 3PL cost records, then produces a reviewable exception register and shipping-economics decision pack.

**Buyer headline:** What did shipping actually cost?

Bring Shopify orders and a supported carrier or 3PL cost export together. See which records match, which remain unresolved, and which conclusions the evidence can support.

NODUS sells a repeatable reconciliation workflow—not generic analytics, a carrier-audit guarantee or an automated shipping-policy optimizer.

Do not claim guaranteed savings, recovered credits, profit improvement, conversion lift, universal carrier coverage, direct UPS/FedEx/USPS invoice APIs, PDF/OCR ingestion, automated carrier invoice polling, policy writes, App Store approval or live subscription billing.

## 2. Initial customer profile

Prioritize Shopify merchants that:
1. Sell physical products and ship parcels.
2. Use an external carrier account or 3PL.
3. Can export carrier/3PL billed charge lines in a supported CSV structure.
4. Have stable invoice/charge identifiers and useful order or tracking references.
5. Review shipping economics repeatedly.
6. Have an owner in finance, operations or ecommerce who can act on the result.

Do not invent a minimum order-volume cutoff. Qualify by current process cost, repeat frequency, export quality and decision impact.

**Economic buyer:** founder/owner, finance lead or operations director.  
**Champion:** fulfillment, ecommerce operations or finance analyst.  
**Data approver:** Shopify store administrator and carrier/3PL account owner.

Disqualify digital-only stores, merchants without usable cost exports, exports without stable charge identity, infrequent low-burden workflows, and buyers whose existing platform already solves the problem.

## 3. The offer

### Founding paid pilot

- **Price:** USD 99/month for the fixed-scope monthly review service; price is a hypothesis until paid demand is demonstrated.
- **Scope:** one store, one reporting period up to 60 days, up to 2,000 orders and one supported carrier/3PL cost CSV.
- **Deliverables:** evidence coverage statement, matched/unmatched and exception register, eligible evidenced shortfall register, currency-separated comparison, export review and written limitations.
- **Payment:** contact-first by email. The public form records interest but does not charge or notify the operator automatically. An authorized adult business operator must confirm scope, issue an invoice through an approved business account and verify cleared funds before work begins.
- **Boundary:** no automatic subscription, carrier claim filing, guaranteed recovery or unsupported financial outcome.

### Deliverables
1. Scope statement: source period, order count, currency, permissions, freshness and exclusions.
2. Evidence coverage: matched, unmatched, duplicate, ambiguous and incomplete records.
3. Reconciliation register: supported order-level calculations with source references.
4. Exception worklist: records requiring merchant or carrier/3PL review.
5. Decision brief: material findings, unknowns, next actions and evidence needed.
6. Exports: merchant-scoped CSV and JSON Decision Pack.
7. Review session: calculations, limitations and operational usefulness.

An assessment is complete only when source scope is explicit; imported rows are accounted for by state; unsupported or ambiguous records are excluded from totals; calculations can be traced; limitations are included; and exports succeed.

Not included: filing carrier claims, contacting carriers for the merchant, accounting advice, guaranteed credits, PDF extraction, custom integrations or unsupported allocation of shared charges.

## 4. Pricing policy

Do not publish a fabricated market price. First establish the recurring job and conservative merchant-accepted value.

NODUS Pro is positioned as a USD 99/month fixed-scope review service, not automated monitoring. Validate the price through real merchant use and paid commitments:
- Separate realized credits, operating-time value and decision value.
- Count credits only when the merchant confirms a posted credit, refund or accounting adjustment.
- Measure time saved from comparable timed tasks and a merchant-approved loaded hourly rate.
- Do not assign speculative dollar values to decision quality.
- Subtract incremental operating costs and required human review.
- Propose a fee only if conservative, recurring, merchant-accepted value is at least 3× the fee.
- If recurring value cannot be demonstrated, do not recommend a subscription.

Any paid tier needs documented limits, support expectations, cancellation terms, billing and entitlement lifecycle tests before checkout is enabled.

## 5. Inbound acquisition system

The acquisition motion is search-led and proof-led. No bulk cold email and no fabricated social proof.

### Public acquisition assets
1. Homepage: one clear problem and one primary CTA.
2. /shipping-cost-reconciliation: search-intent landing page for Shopify shipping cost reconciliation.
3. /demo: synthetic example Decision Pack, labelled synthetic.
4. /buyer-evaluation: qualification, required data and deliverables.
5. /pricing: free diagnostic and USD 99/month fixed-scope monthly review service, with invoice-based payment and explicit cancellation/refund terms.
6. /methodology: exact matching and calculation boundaries.
7. /security and /privacy: data handling, permissions and retention details.
8. CSV template and field-level import documentation.

### Search themes
- Shopify shipping cost reconciliation.
- Shopify shipping revenue vs carrier cost.
- Shopify 3PL invoice reconciliation.
- Shopify carrier cost CSV.
- Shopify shipping margin by order.

Publish useful worked examples with synthetic data. Each should answer a real operator question, show a reproducible calculation, state assumptions and link to the evaluation guide. Avoid thin keyword pages and ranking promises.

### Distribution
- Shopify App Store listing only after required approval and compliant production setup.
- Search-engine-indexable product and methodology pages.
- Technical articles and sample datasets.
- Relevant Shopify, fulfillment and 3PL partner referrals where a real relationship exists.
- Transparent founder/product channels.
- Public demo and downloadable CSV template.

### Funnel
Qualified discovery → product page → sample pack → fit check → evaluation request → authorized store/data approval → completed assessment → merchant decision → second-period repeat → paid commitment.

A page view is not a lead; a lead is not a qualified buyer; a purchase request is not a cleared payment.

## 6. Sales qualification

Ask:
1. How do you compare Shopify checkout shipping revenue with billed carrier or 3PL cost today?
2. How often do you do this, and who owns it?
3. How long does one cycle take?
4. Which carrier/3PL systems produce the export?
5. Does each charge have a stable invoice/charge ID?
6. What proportion of rows include an order or tracking reference?
7. How are split shipments, adjustments, voids, credits and shared fees handled?
8. Which decisions or investigations are delayed by the current process?
9. What would make a result trustworthy enough to act on?
10. Does the same work recur next month?

Understand the existing workflow before giving a product tour. If the export cannot support reliable matching, say so before requesting store access.

## 7. Objection handling

| Objection | Accurate response |
|---|---|
| “Our 3PL already gives us reports.” | Compare the actual report with the Shopify order-level questions. If it already answers them, NODUS may add no value. |
| “Can you recover carrier overcharges?” | NODUS can surface supported discrepancies and evidence. It does not file claims or guarantee credits. |
| “Do you connect directly to UPS/FedEx/USPS?” | Current supported intake is merchant-provided CSV. Direct invoice APIs are not live. |
| “Does it forecast profit or conversion?” | No. Historical policy replay compares stored baskets; it does not model demand response or full profit. |
| “Can you monitor invoices automatically?” | Shopify order refresh exists; automated carrier invoice polling and alerts are not offered. |
| “How much will it save us?” | We do not estimate savings before reviewing merchant data. Confirmed credits, identified exposure and measured operating time stay separate. |
| “What does it cost after the assessment?” | No recurring price is promised before repeat value is demonstrated. |

## 8. Commercial metrics

**Acquisition:** organic impressions/clicks, qualified sessions, demo-to-guide clicks, evaluation requests, qualified evaluation rate.

**Activation:** authorized store connection, first successful sync, first supported cost import, import acceptance rate, time to first reviewable result.

**Product value:** match coverage, unresolved/ambiguous rates, review time, merchant-confirmed actionable findings, posted credits separately, repeat assessment completion.

**Commercial:** explicit willingness-to-pay evidence, paid conversion after authorized payment workflow exists, recurring retention, support/manual-review cost per merchant.

Do not optimize for sign-ups that do not become completed, useful evaluations.

## 9. Launch gates

A public marketing preview may be exposed only after an operator approves the content and confirms no merchant workspace or private data is reachable.

A commercial service additionally requires:
- compliant commercial hosting and an authorized business operator;
- production database, secrets, migrations and backup/restore verification;
- Shopify Partner app configuration, OAuth, webhooks, permissions and network allowlisting;
- production security, privacy, retention and deletion review;
- working business-owned enquiry/support channel;
- real-store installation and tenant-isolation verification;
- recurring carrier evidence workflow if recurring monitoring is sold;
- billing, entitlements, cancellation and support operations before accepting subscriptions.

**Vercel Hobby/Free is not the commercial production host for a revenue-generating service.** It can only be considered for a non-commercial preview after protection and exposure are deliberately reviewed.

## 10. Ownership and truthful status

An authorized adult/business operator must own commercial accounts, contracts, payment collection, legal identity, tax and data-protection obligations, support escalation and production billing. Never put credentials, payment details or customer data in public GitHub issues.

No testimonial, merchant logo, savings claim, App Store approval, security certification or customer count may be published without evidence and permission.

**Commercial success:** a qualified merchant completes the free diagnostic, chooses the monthly review service, receives a useful decision brief, returns for a later period and makes an authorized payment for the recurring deliverable.
