# NODUS Commercial Offer

The current commercial product definition is maintained in [COMMERCIAL_PRODUCT.md](COMMERCIAL_PRODUCT.md).

NODUS is positioned as **Shipping Margin Control for Shopify**. Its initial buyer is a physical-goods merchant with recurring carrier or 3PL costs, flat-rate or free-shipping policies, and usable cost exports.

The offer is an evidence-backed workflow:
1. Connect authorized Shopify order data.
2. Import carrier or 3PL cost CSV evidence.
3. Reconcile supported order and cost records.
4. Review incomplete, unmatched and duplicate evidence.
5. Compare flat-rate and free-shipping alternatives against historical orders.
6. Export a reproducible decision pack.

The initial pricing hypothesis is **$29/month per store**. This is not validated willingness to pay and is not purchasable until Shopify App Pricing, plan configuration, entitlement enforcement and the complete billing lifecycle are verified.

Do not claim direct carrier APIs, automated invoice polling, automatic policy changes, guaranteed savings, App Store approval or full accounting profit. The product's recurring value depends on merchants importing new cost evidence and using the workflow again in later periods.

See [COMMERCIAL_PRODUCT.md](COMMERCIAL_PRODUCT.md) for the buyer definition, differentiation, pricing scope, acquisition plan, metrics and release gates.
